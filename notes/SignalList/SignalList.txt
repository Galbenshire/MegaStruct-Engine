==== BOSS RELATED ====

=== SIGNAL_BOSS_FIGHTSTART () ===
-- A boss fight has started

=== SIGNAL_BOSS_DEATH (boss) ===
-- A boss has just died
- @param {prtBoss}  boss  The boss instance


==== PLAYER RELATED ====

=== SIGNAL_PLAYER_HURT (player) ===
-- The player was just hurt
- @param {prtPlayer}  player  The hurt player entity

=== SIGNAL_PLAYER_INPUT (player, input) ===
-- A snapshot of the player's input
- @param {prtPlayer}  player  The player entity
- @param {InputMap}  input  The player's input map

=== SIGNAL_PLAYER_PALETTE_UPDATE (player) ===
-- The player's palette has been updated
- @param {prtPlayer}  player  The player entity

=== SIGNAL_PLAYER_SHOT (player, projectile) ===
-- The player has just fired a weapon
- @param {prtPlayer}  player  The player entity that just fired a shot
- @param {instance}  projectile  The projectile being fired

=== SIGNAL_DISPEL_UTILITIES () ===
